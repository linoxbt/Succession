# Succession product demo narration

## 1. Verifiable memory ownership

AI agents become valuable through experience: the preferences they learn, the relationships they build, the commitments they carry, and the history behind every decision. Succession turns that Sibyl memory into verifiable, transferable property.

## 2. Live operator view

The Succession dashboard gives operators a clear view of service health and settlement on Base Sepolia. Beneath the key indicators is a live provenance ledger built from completed transfers, so evidence is visible without exposing wallet keys in the browser.

## 3. Inspect the provenance

Open any verified sale to inspect its certificate. This transfer moved forty-nine records from agent six ninety-two to successor agent six ninety-three. The package includes identity, relationships, preferences, history, commitments, and learned behaviors. Wallets, listing, version, settlement time, Merkle root, and the BaseScan transaction remain available for independent review.

## 4. Begin with inventory

The command line starts with an explicit inventory. Here, the origin agent has sellable records in all six Sibyl categories, while a private relationship record remains withheld. Operators choose exactly what can leave the source store before creating an offer.

## 5. Prove before listing

The prove command exports the selected package, imports it into an isolated temporary store, and derives the destination root again. Matching roots establish deterministic portability before a buyer pays or a content key is released.

## 6. Three independent roles

The seller signs the Merkle root and publishes an encrypted package. The buyer reviews the commitment and funds one USDC of escrow. A separately controlled evaluator decrypts into an isolated store, checks the seller signature, and independently derives the same root.

## 7. Atomic settlement

Only a successful verdict settles the sale. The contract releases payment, transfers the ERC-eight-thousand-and-four identity, and makes the content key claimable. The buyer then imports transactionally and verifies the package in its own destination store.

## 8. Real Base Sepolia evidence

This is a completed Base Sepolia transfer. The committed root and delivered root match exactly. Forty-nine records moved across six categories, and the acquisition certificate names both the origin and successor agents. The settlement transaction is permanent, public evidence of the handover.

## 9. Built for operations

Succession also documents installation, every command, wallet separation, finality policy, and two-host recovery. Durable seller and buyer journals make interrupted work replay-safe without duplicate imports or duplicate settlement.

## 10. Carry intelligence forward

Succession gives agent memory a chain of custody: committed before delivery, verified in isolation, settled atomically, and proven at the destination. Preserve the experience. Transfer the identity. Carry intelligence forward.
